## Eagle of the Month 1.0.2

Keeps Google Sheets connections portable when moving to another Mac. Transfer the app and your private workspace together, then choose the workspace on the new machine.

- Connect or change the Google Sheet URL and tab from the app.
- Import service-account credentials into the private workspace so they travel with it.
- Find workspace credentials independently of the launch directory and use the workspace Photos folder by default.
- Show clearer instructions when sheet access or credentials need attention.

Requires macOS 15 or later and Apple Silicon.
